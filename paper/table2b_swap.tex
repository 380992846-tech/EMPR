\section{The \emph{swap} probe}
\label{app:swap}
The \emph{swap} perturbation exchanges two distinct physical quantities, producing a
physically inconsistent problem. Most benchmark problems contain quantities of a
single type or carry no units, so only a minority of items admit such a swap. Because
the resulting sample is small and its percentages therefore unstable, we report the
numbers for completeness and draw \emph{no} quantitative conclusion from them; they
are excluded from Table~\ref{tab:sensitivity} precisely so that they are not read as
comparable to the other two probes.
